Updated the digital standards to define Open Sans as the MarinOS body/user-interface font and to require local first-party font assets instead of runtime font/CDN calls.

Affected standards:

- `brand/typography.md`
- `product-design/runtime-dependencies.md`
- `accessibility/testing.md`
