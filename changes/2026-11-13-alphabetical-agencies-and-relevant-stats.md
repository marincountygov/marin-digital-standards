# 2026-11-13 — Agencies A to Z, and Stats follows the filter

Added two rules to the interaction design page, in "Filtering, sorting and saying what is shown":

- Service areas, departments, divisions, agencies and other County bodies run A to Z by name in every list, table, card grid, chart and filter group, and a list of them opens sorted by name. A chart titled as a ranking may order by amount. A page that reproduces a printed book keeps the book's order.
- Stats shows only what is relevant to the filter. A summary of the whole appears only while nothing narrows the selection; the year is not a filter.
