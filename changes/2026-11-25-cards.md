# 2026-11-25 — Cards

Added to the interaction design page, "Cards":

- Every card is the same surface; an app does not redraw it.
- A card is for a thing a person picks; text a person reads goes in a plain box.
- A card's name is 1.25rem and bold, its text 1rem, its small lines 0.875rem.
- A card has no badges except a status.
- A list of cards is one column, or two or three that become one on a narrow screen.

Affected standards:

- `product-design/interaction-design.md`

Implementation: Marin UI 1.42.0 (`docs/components.md`, "Cards") and App Shell 2.7.0 (`audit-cards.py`). Seven apps that carried their own copies of the card styles now use the shared ones. Marin Docs' `.card`, Marin Brand's logo cards, two Marin Learn panels and Meeting Meter's idle card are marked as exceptions to move later.
