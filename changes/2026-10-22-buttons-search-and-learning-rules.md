# 2026-10-22 — Buttons, search placement, and two learning-content rules

Added a Buttons section to the interaction design page: one quiet look for secondary buttons, icon-only buttons named with `aria-label` and `title`, related buttons clustered, and often-pressed pairs (Previous and Next) spaced apart with 44 by 44 pixel targets. Added to the filter panel rule: the text search stays visible outside the panel, the panel has a visible title, and on narrow screens its button sits beside the tabs. Added two writing rules to learning content: no acronym in a description unless the heading spells it out, and no general assumptions.

Affected standards:

- `product-design/interaction-design.md`
- `product-design/responsive-design.md`
- `content-design/learning-content.md`

First applied in `marin-reach`, `marin-mentions`, and `marin-learn`; the styles are in Marin App Shell 1.15.0 and 1.16.0.
