# 2026-10-23 — When buttons keep words, solid buttons, and the other button kinds

Rewrote the Buttons section of the interaction design page. Icon-only now applies to repeated utility actions. Words stay on the main action of a screen, on dialog buttons, and on anything without a familiar icon. A screen has at most one solid button, for its main result. Danger actions turn red on hover. Tabs, filter chips and sortable column headers are named as their own kinds of button, and a menu button is an icon button with a caret.

Affected standards:

- `product-design/interaction-design.md`

Drives the button clean-up across the MarinOS apps; see `marin-app-shell` 1.17.0.
