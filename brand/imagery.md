# Imagery

The public Identity Style Guide doesn't cover photography and iconography in as much depth as logo/color/typography — this document captures what is established and stays intentionally thin rather than inventing rules the guide doesn't support.

## Photos

Use official photos or approved imagery when available. Images should support the message, not appear generic or purely decorative — a stock photo that could illustrate any County page isn't doing its job. Don't crowd a layout with competing photos and logos.

## Icons

Keep icon style consistent within a single asset or product — mixing icon families reads as unpolished even when each icon individually looks fine.

## What this doesn't cover

Photography usage rights, stock-photo sourcing policy, and detailed iconography systems aren't established in the source guide. If a specific need arises that isn't covered here, confirm with the responsible County team rather than assuming a rule exists.
