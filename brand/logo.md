# Logo

## Source file

The official County logo is available at `https://www.marincounty.gov/themes/custom/marin_county/logo.png`. Store approved artwork locally in the project (e.g. `assets/logo.png`) and reference the local file — don't hotlink the County website's asset from another app. If no approved asset is available in a project, use text branding ("County of Marin") instead of generating fake logo art.

## Hierarchy

1. **Primary Logo** — Civic Center symbol plus County of Marin logotype. First-choice representation of the County.
2. **Secondary Logo** — reproportioned for smaller or constrained spaces where "County of Marin" must stay legible.
3. **Logotypes** — typographic-only representations for situations where size or logo clutter calls for simpler identification.
4. **Symbol alone** — select approved applications only.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Use provided official artwork — never recreate the logo.
- Never alter, stretch, distort, rotate, recolor, crop, outline, add shadows, or otherwise modify the artwork.
- Maintain clear space around the logo; never overlap it with other logos, graphics, photos, copy, or decorative elements.
- Use the black-line version in ordinary applications; reversed white artwork only on dark/black backgrounds; other approved color variants only for special applications.
- Never use the County logo as a watermark, background texture, decorative pattern, or repeated motif unless an official template already does so.

## Minimum size

- Primary Logo: minimum width 1.25 inches, measured from the left edge of the dome to the dotted end of the horizon line.
- Secondary Logo: minimum width 0.75 inches, measured from the left edge of the dome to the right margin of the logotype.
- Digital equivalents must preserve legibility — if the logo becomes unclear at a given size, switch to the Secondary Logo or a logotype rather than scaling below readable size.

## Color logo use

Permitted color treatments: the approved circular seal, square patch, gold symbol, and all-gold version. The gold specification is fixed, not approximated:

- PMS 7405 uncoated / PMS 7406 coated
- CMYK: 11, 28, 90, 0
- RGB: 229, 181, 59
- HEX: `#e5b53b`

Metallic gold is print-only: PMS 8643 or a matching matte metallic foil.

## Placement by context

- **County homepage**: Secondary Logo in the upper-left area.
- **Department websites**: County Primary Logo in the standard page footer, linking back to the County homepage, placed in the bottom-right corner (black or white), preferably with a dotted line to its left.
- **Social media avatars**: Secondary Logo or Secondary Logotype, to preserve legibility of "County of Marin" at small size — never a complex full layout as an avatar.
- **Video**: County or department logo at the beginning and end; use the Primary Logo when no department logo exists. Third-party vendor logos may appear at the end alongside the County Primary Logo, but must not dominate County identity when the video represents County work.

## Co-branding and departments

A department with its own recognized logo or graphic style may express it, while preserving County family identity. When co-branding guidance is unclear, request or confirm official department guidance rather than guessing.
